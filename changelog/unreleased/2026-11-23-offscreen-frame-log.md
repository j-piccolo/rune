- `tooling::FrameLog` (frame-profile): the frame log for a runtime its app steps itself, an offscreen stage with its own clock and input. Each frame draws as an overlay draws it (whole, over transparent) into `render::WINDOW_FORMAT`, the format a Linux window's surface takes, and is written as a headless run writes its frames, with the GPU's own time for it from timestamps (`gpu_time_ms`) and its clock (`gpu_mhz`) where the device has `TIMESTAMP_QUERY_INSIDE_ENCODERS`, which devices are now asked for. `RUNE_DRAW_DUMP` dumps its draw lists.
