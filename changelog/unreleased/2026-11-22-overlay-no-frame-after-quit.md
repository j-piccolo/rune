- Overlays: a quit no longer waits on the compositor. An overlay draws no frame once the app is quitting, and a layer or XWayland surface hidden by a quit that ends in a fast exit leaves its swap chain to the process's end instead of destroying it. A lock surface drew a frame after its session lock was released and the present waited for a frame callback the compositor no longer sent, so the quit never ended; destroying its swap chain waited a second or more for the same reason.
