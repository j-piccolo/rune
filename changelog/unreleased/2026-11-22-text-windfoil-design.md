- **Text: `FontSmoothing::WindfoilDesign` draws every face from its design outline.** Under `WindfoilChromium`, off macOS, a face that carries TrueType hints runs them for each glyph at each size it is drawn at, 20 to 40 us a glyph, and Inter's static faces (the embedded ones included) carry ttfautohint's: a page of text in sizes or weights not drawn before spent 2 to 5 ms of the frame that first showed it in the font's program (a 1080p page of card rails with 56,000 channels behind it, 9.9 ms to 4.6 ms). `WindfoilDesign` is `WindfoilChromium` without them: one outline a glyph at every size, heights and horizontal stems where the design puts them, a little softer than the hinted outline in small text at 1x. `WindfoilChromium` is unchanged and stays the default.
