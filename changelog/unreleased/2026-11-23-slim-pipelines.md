- The commonest quads draw on slim pipelines of their own (`render/slim.rs`) where the window's or a layer's pipeline would draw them: a plain atlas glyph (no outline, subpixel, rotation or zoom), a borderless box with circular corners and no fade, a whole-pixel square box inside its clip (one draw, no bands), a box's middle, a linear gradient's middle that lies inside its stops (its colour interpolated between the vertices), a radial gradient's middle, and a layer composited upright, 1:1, unfaded and not additive. Each carries fewer varyings and skips the work the uber shader does for what these instances don't have. Rune draws a frame's quads without allocating its pipeline choices. No pixel changes beyond 1/255.
