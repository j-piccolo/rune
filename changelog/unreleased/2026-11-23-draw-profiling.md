- Frame-profile builds can leave quads undrawn by the path that draws them (`RUNE_SKIP_MODE`) or by their rect (`RUNE_SKIP_RECT=x,y,w,h;...`), as `RUNE_SKIP_KIND` does by kind, to say what each costs a GPU; `RUNE_DRAW_TRACE` prints each span `draw_quads` draws with its kinds and quad modes, and the `RUNE_PINNED` report counts pipeline binds and draws. None of it costs anything when unset.
