- Within a run of boxes and plain glyphs (between layers, effects, pictures, gradients and shadows), the renderer draws the boxes that split, then the other boxes, then the glyphs, moving an instance ahead only past ones it doesn't overlap, so a grid or a list (a month's day cells, their dots and their labels) draws in a few batched draws rather than switching pipelines cell by cell.
