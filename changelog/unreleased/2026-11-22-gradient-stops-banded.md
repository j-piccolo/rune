- Where the uber shader compiles from WGSL (Vulkan, GL), a linear gradient's stop segment rasterizes only the band it draws in. Each segment covered its whole element and shaded zero wherever another segment draws, so a five-stop gradient shaded its element four times to colour it once. The fragment stage is unchanged and the pixels are the same; the shipped Metal and DX12 bytecode still draws whole quads. `RUNE_GRADIENT_BANDS=0` compiles the shader unbanded.
- `RUNE_PINNED_WGSL=1` builds the pipelines pinned to one kind (rects, shadows, glyphs and gradients) from WGSL on Vulkan and GL, where they were made only from the shipped DX12 bytecode. Off by default: the smaller shaders took a tenth off the GPU's time on scrolling pages on the one machine measured, and the draw per run of a kind cost its CPU about as much.
