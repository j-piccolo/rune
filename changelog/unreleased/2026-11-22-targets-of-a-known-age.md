- `Renderer::arm_aged_target`: a host whose buffers come back holding a frame of a known age (`EGL_EXT_buffer_age`, a swap chain whose images keep their pixels) says so, and a partial frame then copies the kept frame out only where the frames since redrew it, not all of it. No window host calls it yet; `RUNE_TARGET_AGE=<n>` has a headless run draw into `n` targets in turn and say so, to model one.
- Frame-profile builds: `RUNE_DRAW_DUMP=<dir>` writes every headless frame's instance list and redraw plan, for tools that count what a frame asks of a GPU from the draw list itself.
