- Fewer groups draw into a layer of their own. A node that clips itself rounded, or square, clear of an ancestor's rounded corners (and paints nothing outside its rect) needs only its own mask, as does a single-line input clear of them: a month grid's day cells inside a rounded panel each opened a layer for the panel's corners. A fading group whose children paint a single thing between them (a label, an icon or a plain fill, with nothing over it) passes its opacity down to that paint, as a lone node does. Glyph edges and fills that drew through a layer move by up to 3/255 where they did: they draw as the same content draws anywhere without one.
