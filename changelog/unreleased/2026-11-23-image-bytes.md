- The image atlas holds pictures' sRGB-encoded bytes as they came (`Rgba8Unorm`), read as they are where a frame blends sRGB-encoded (the default) and decoded in the shader where it blends linear. A picture's texel cost a hardware decode and three `pow`s to encode it again: a full-screen picture's frame took half the GPU time it did on a small GPU. Pixels move by at most 1/255 (the round trip's rounding).
