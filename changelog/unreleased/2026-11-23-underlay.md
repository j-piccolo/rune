- `El::underlay`: an opaque picture covering the window (a lock screen's wallpaper) that the compositor shows under the window instead of the window drawing it. While it rests whole (fully opaque, unclipped, one texel a device pixel, with only plain shapes under it), a layer-shell or session-lock surface shows it as a subsurface below itself (`wl_subcompositor`, `wl_shm`, `wp_viewporter`), made once per picture, and the frame leaves out it and everything under it (`Renderer::set_underlay_start`); fading, moving, or with a texel not opaque, it draws in the window as before, and the subsurface goes in the same commit. The frame log (`frame-profile`) leaves it out as such a surface does. `RUNE_UNDERLAY=0` draws it in the window.
