- `ImageCache::load_within(bytes, size, fit)`: decodes a picture shrunk on the worker to what a box of `size` device pixels shows of it (the smallest size that still covers the box for `SurfaceFit::Fill`, the largest that fits for `SurfaceFit::Fit`), averaging by area in linear light as the mips do, and never enlarged. A picture a server sends at 667 × 1000 for a 340 × 510 card holds 0.5 MB of decoded pixels and atlas space instead of 2.7 MB. `Image::downgrade` gives a `WeakImage`, which has the image back for as long as anything else holds it: an app's cache can let go of decoded pixels nothing draws and still find the ones that are drawn without hashing their bytes again.
