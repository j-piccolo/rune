- A box 32 device pixels or more each way (not banded, with no per-side border or fade stops in its clip), a picture and a radial gradient's stop segment draw as their middle and four bands around it, split in the vertex stage (`SPLIT_VERTICES`): the middle is where the shape and its clip cover every pixel whole, and it draws with a small pipeline of its kind's (`variant::middle_wgsl`: a box returns its fill, a picture its texel, a segment its colour), made on a thread from WGSL where the uber shader is, for the window and for its layers; the uber shader draws it until then, and elsewhere. A big ring (a border with nothing inside) draws as its four corners and four edges only as deep as the ring. The uber shader skips coverage and clip deep inside a box or a clip, and a picture drawn at its own size on whole pixels reads one texel a pixel. No pixel changes. The vertex stage changed, so the shipped Metal and DX12 bytecode is stale until regenerated (`regen_dxbc`, the metallib build): those platforms compile the WGSL meanwhile.
- The pipelines pinned to one kind are built from WGSL on Vulkan and GL by default, pictures' too, and a span whose kinds alternate still draws a run of eight or more of one kind, or an instance 32 pixels or more each way, with its kind's: on a small GPU (Intel's 24-EU UHD on an N100) they took a fifth off a bar's GPU time for a few percent more CPU. `RUNE_PINNED_WGSL=0` turns them off.
